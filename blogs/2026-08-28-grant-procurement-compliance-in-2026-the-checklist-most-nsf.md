This federal grant compliance checklist brings the US federal framework and Horizon Europe’s grant preparation requirements into one category-by-category checklist. Most existing guidance covers the Uniform Guidance or Horizon Europe, but rarely both, creating compliance gaps for institutions managing awards across frameworks. Very few institutions manage only one, leaving real gaps in an audit.
