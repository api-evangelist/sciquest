Procurement governance and compliance is the framework of policies, controls, and oversight that shapes how organizations buy, manage suppliers, and manage risk. As AI takes on a growing share of procurement decisions, getting that framework right from day one is critical. Procurement Magazine’s August 2026 issue features JAGGAER CEO Andrew Roszko.
