JAGGAER and Precoro serve different scales of procurement complexity: enterprise source-to-pay (S2P) versus mid-market procure-to-pay (P2P). JAGGAER unifies strategic sourcing, contract management, direct and indirect spend, and supplier intelligence in one system. Precoro is a procure-to-pay and AP automation platform for mid-sized companies consolidating indirect purchasing.
