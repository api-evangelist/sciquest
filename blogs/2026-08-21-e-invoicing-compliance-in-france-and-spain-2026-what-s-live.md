E-invoicing compliance in France and Spain is live in some places, imminent in others, and delayed in Spain’s biggest system. France has required electronic invoicing for public-sector suppliers since January 1, 2020 (economie.gouv.fr). France’s B2B mandate takes effect September 1, 2026 (economie.gouv.fr).
